Numerical Study on the Heat Transfer Characteristics for Laminar Flow with Staggered Triangular Pin Fin in Arrays

2024 – 2025

Undergraduate Thesis · Supervisor: Prof. Dr. Md. Shahidul Islam, Department of Mechanical Engineering, KUET

For my KUET thesis, I used CFD to compare laminar heat transfer and flow behavior in staggered arrays of triangular pin fins. My work included setting up the simulations, monitoring the solutions, and comparing the thermal and flow results.

The tandem-cylinder study asks a related question at the scale of two bodies: how does the first cylinder’s wake affect the second?
