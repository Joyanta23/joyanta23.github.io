Tandem Cylinder CFD

I ran transient Fluent simulations of two heated triangles in line. At a gap of three cylinder widths and Reynolds number 120, the 30° triangles transferred the most heat per unit depth, while the 90° triangles had the highest average Nusselt number.

Titanium Alloy Machine Learning
