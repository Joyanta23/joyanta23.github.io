Wake Interference and Heat Transfer of Heated Tandem Triangular Cylinders: Effects of Apex Angle, Gap Ratio, and Reynolds Number

2026 – Present

Computational Fluid Dynamics · Convective Heat Transfer

  • Conducting two-dimensional transient CFD simulations of two heated triangular cylinders arranged in tandem inside a channel, using ANSYS Fluent 2023 R1.
  • Studying the coupled influence of apex angle, cylinder spacing, and Reynolds number on wake behavior, drag, lift, and heat transfer.
  • Established stable periodic simulations and time-averaging workflows for the upstream and downstream Nusselt numbers and force coefficients, supported by mesh- and time-step-independence studies and comparison with a published single-cylinder benchmark.

Machine Learning Prediction of Resistivity and Hardness in Isothermally Aged β Titanium Alloys: Added Value of Measured Resistivity

2026 – Present

Supervisor: Prof. Dr. Md. Ashraful Islam (Google Scholar), Department of Mechanical Engineering, KUET

I digitized published aging curves and assembled 836 resistivity records and 568 hardness records, including 349 matched aging conditions.

Methods and model results

I matched measurements by alloy and processing conditions, selected models by five-fold cross-validation on training rows, and evaluated them on held-out rows. ExtraTrees achieved test R² of 0.9640 for resistivity and 0.9703 for hardness. I used SHAP to examine how inputs contributed to the model predictions.
