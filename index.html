About Me

I completed my B.Sc. in Mechanical Engineering at Khulna University of Engineering & Technology (KUET), Bangladesh, in December 2025. I work on two areas: CFD and heat transfer, and machine learning for engineering materials.

My undergraduate thesis was a numerical study of laminar-flow heat transfer with triangular pin-fin arrays. I am now running transient CFD simulations in ANSYS Fluent to study the wake and heat transfer of two heated triangular cylinders placed in tandem.

I also work on aged titanium alloys. I built a dataset of resistivity and hardness values taken from published studies and trained machine-learning models, including XGBoost, to predict these properties. I use SHAP to see which features drive the predictions.
